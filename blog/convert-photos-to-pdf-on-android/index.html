How to Convert Photos to PDF on Android

Want to send several photos as one neat document? Your Android phone makes it easy to prepare images for sharing, printing, school or work.

Why convert photos to PDF?

A PDF can keep several pictures together in one file. Instead of sending many separate image attachments, you can share one document that is easier to store, print and organise.

Method 1: Use a free online image-to-PDF tool

  1. Open Chrome or another browser on your Android phone.
  2. Visit the ImageToPDF.io converter.
  3. Select the option to choose images from your phone.
  4. Choose the photos you want to include. If prompted, allow access to the selected files.
  5. Check the selected images and arrange their order if the tool offers that option.
  6. Start the conversion and download the PDF when it is ready.
  7. Open your Downloads folder to find the saved document.

Browser features can vary by phone and browser version. If selecting multiple photos at once is unavailable, try selecting them again through your phone's file picker.

Method 2: Check your phone's built-in options

Some Android phones offer a Print option in the Gallery, Photos or file-viewing app. Open an image, tap the menu, and look for Print or Share options. If Print is available, select Save as PDF when offered. The exact steps depend on your phone model and installed apps.

How to combine multiple photos into one PDF

If you need one PDF containing several photos, choose all the images before converting rather than creating a separate PDF for each picture. Review their order first, especially when the photos are pages of a document, receipts or school notes.

Tips for a better PDF

  • Check the order: Put the first page or most important photo first.
  • Use clear images: Avoid blurry or very dark photos when possible.
  • Keep the file manageable: Very large photos can create a larger PDF.
  • Review the result: Open the PDF and check that every image is included and readable.
  • Protect private information: Be careful when uploading sensitive identity documents or personal photos to any online service.

Do you need to install an app?

Not always. You can use a browser-based converter without installing another app. A built-in print-to-PDF option may also be available on your phone. Choose the method that works best for your device and the privacy needs of your files.

Frequently asked questions

Can I convert photos to PDF on Android for free?

Yes. You can use a free online converter or a built-in Save as PDF option if your phone supports it.

Can I put several photos into one PDF?

Yes. Select multiple photos in a converter that supports batch processing, then create one PDF file.

Where is my downloaded PDF saved?

Usually, it appears in the Downloads folder. You can also check your browser's download history.

Will the photos keep their original quality?

The result depends on the images and conversion settings. Open the PDF after conversion to make sure the pictures remain clear enough for your needs.
