How to Convert Images to PDF for Email Attachments

Need to send photos, scanned documents, receipts, or forms by email? Converting your images into a PDF can make them easier to organize and share. Instead of attaching several separate image files, you can combine multiple images into one document.

Why Send Images as a PDF?

A PDF is a convenient format for sharing documents with employers, schools, offices, clients, and other recipients. It can keep multiple pages together in one file and make a collection of scanned pages easier to review.

  • Combine several images into one document.
  • Keep scanned pages organized in one file.
  • Make receipts, forms, and photographs easier to share.
  • Reduce the number of separate attachments in an email.

Step 1: Choose the Images You Want to Send

Find the JPG, JPEG, PNG, or WEBP images you want to include. Check that the pictures are clear, correctly oriented, and arranged in the order you want the recipient to see them.

Step 2: Open the Image to PDF Converter

Visit our free online Image to PDF Converter. You can use it in a web browser on a compatible phone, tablet, or computer.

Step 3: Add Your Images

Select the images you want to convert using the tool's image-selection option. If you have several pages, select all the required images and check their order before creating your PDF.

Step 4: Create Your PDF

Follow the converter's on-screen instructions to turn your selected images into a PDF. When the conversion is complete, download the resulting file to your device.

Step 5: Attach the PDF to Your Email

Open your email service and start a new message. Enter the recipient's email address, add a subject, and write a short message. Use the attachment option to select the PDF you downloaded, then wait for the upload to finish.

Before sending, make sure you selected the correct PDF and that its file size is accepted by your email service.

What If the PDF Is Too Large?

Email services usually have attachment-size limits, and the exact limit depends on the provider. If your PDF is too large to attach, try using fewer images, choosing smaller original images, or using a suitable PDF compression tool. You can also use your email provider's supported cloud-sharing option when appropriate.

Tips for Better Email Attachments

  • Use a clear filename, such as Application-Documents.pdf.
  • Check that every page is readable before sending.
  • Arrange the pages in a logical order.
  • Open the downloaded PDF to confirm that it contains the intended images.
  • Double-check the recipient's address, especially when sending private documents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I combine multiple images into one PDF?

Yes. Use an image-to-PDF converter that supports multiple images, select the images you need, and create a single PDF file.

Can I convert images to PDF on my phone?

Yes. You can use a compatible online converter in your phone's browser. The exact steps may vary depending on your device and browser.

Will converting images to PDF reduce the file size?

Not necessarily. The resulting PDF size depends on the original images and how the converter processes them. You may need a separate compression tool if the file is too large for email.

Is a PDF better than sending separate photos?

A PDF can be more convenient when you need to send several related images together. Separate image attachments may be preferable when the recipient needs each original image individually.
