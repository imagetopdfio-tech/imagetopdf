How to Convert JPG to PDF in Google Drive – Step-by-Step Guide

If you have a JPG image and need it in PDF format, you can use Google Drive together with Google Docs. This method does not require separate PDF conversion software and can be useful when your image is already stored in Google Drive.

In this guide, you will learn how to upload a JPG to Google Drive, open it with Google Docs, and download the resulting document as a PDF.

Can You Convert JPG to PDF Using Google Drive?

Yes. Google Drive can be used with Google Docs to create a PDF from a JPG image. The basic process is to upload the JPG to Google Drive, open the image with Google Docs, and then download the document as a PDF file.

How to Convert JPG to PDF in Google Drive

1. Open Google Drive

Open Google Drive in your web browser and sign in to your Google account. Then open the folder where you want to store the JPG image.

2. Upload Your JPG Image

Click New in Google Drive and select File upload. Choose the JPG or JPEG image from your computer and wait for the upload to finish.

3. Open the JPG With Google Docs

Find the uploaded JPG image in Google Drive. Right-click the file, select Open with, and then choose Google Docs.

Google Docs will create a new document containing the image. You can adjust the image size or document layout if needed.

4. Download the Document as a PDF

In Google Docs, select File, then choose Download and select PDF Document (.pdf).

Google Docs will download the document to your device as a PDF file.

How to Convert Multiple JPG Images to One PDF

If you have several JPG images, you can insert them into the same Google Docs document and arrange them in the order you want. You can then download the entire document as one PDF file.

This method can be useful for combining photos, scanned pages, receipts or other image files into a single document.
