How to Convert JPG to PDF in Google Drive – Step-by-Step Guide

Need to turn a JPG image into a PDF without installing special software? You can use Google Drive together with Google Docs to convert a JPG image into a PDF document. This guide explains the process step by step.

Can You Convert JPG to PDF Using Google Drive?

Yes. Google Drive can be used together with Google Docs to create a PDF from a JPG image. You upload the JPG to Google Drive, open it with Google Docs, and then download the document as a PDF.

How to Convert JPG to PDF in Google Drive

Step 1: Open Google Drive

Open Google Drive and sign in to your Google account. Go to the folder where you want to upload your JPG image.

Step 2: Upload Your JPG Image

Click New in Google Drive and select File upload. Choose the JPG image from your computer and wait for the upload to finish.

Step 3: Open the JPG With Google Docs

Find the uploaded JPG file in Google Drive. Right-click the image, choose Open with, and select Google Docs.

Google Docs will create a new document containing the image. You can adjust the document layout if necessary.

Step 4: Download the JPG as a PDF

In Google Docs, click File, select Download, and then choose PDF Document (.pdf).

Your document will be downloaded as a PDF file.
